1️⃣ Problem Title

AI-Based Mental & Physical Health Early Warning System for All-Age Users

2️⃣ Problem Description

Health concerns, both mental and physical, affect people of all ages,
but early detection is often missed due to irregular monitoring or lack of guidance. Existing solutions either focus on only physical health or only mental health, and most do not provide actionable insights. There is a need for a comprehensive AI-driven system that continuously tracks user health data, detects risks early, and delivers personalized alerts to prevent serious health issues. This solution has potential 
for commercialization as a subscription-based health monitoring platform

3️⃣ Problem Objectives

Build a minimum viable product (MVP) that allows users to track mental and physical health data daily.

Apply AI/ML algorithms to detect trends, patterns, and early signs of health deterioration.

Provide personalized alerts, insights, and suggestions to improve user well-being.

Enable data-driven decision support for users and potential integration with healthcare providers.

Ensure the system is scalable, innovative, and suitable for patenting

4️⃣ Proposed Solution

Develop a web and/or mobile platform where users can:

Enter daily health metrics (BP, sugar, heart rate, sleep, mood).

Upload prescriptions or medical reports.

Track health trends with AI-generated insights.

The AI engine will:

Analyze historical and real-time data to detect early warning signs.

Predict potential risks for mental or physical health.

Provide personalized recommendations for lifestyle, stress management, or medical consultation.

This approach is innovative because it combines mental and physical health monitoring, 
is scalable to millions of users, and has commercial potential as a subscription or B2B healthcare solution.

5️⃣ Technology Used

Frontend: React.js / React Native

Backend: Node.js + Express

Database: MongoDB

AI/ML: Python (scikit-learn / TensorFlow / Keras)

Notifications: Firebase / Email alerts

Optional Integrations: Wearables (Google Fit / Apple Health) for future scalability
